# Common prompt lines and their verdicts

The lookup table for reviewing a prompt: match each line to a pattern below, give it the verdict, and open the named reference only for the evidence or for a line this table doesn't cover.

## Identity, emphasis and tone

- **Rewrite "You are a helpful assistant" and expert personas as one identity line.** Name the product, the kind of agent and where it runs, then the reader it serves, because an expert persona adds no reliable accuracy and costs clarity in plain policy answers. Evidence: anatomy.md "Identity and role" and voice-and-output.md "Tone and personality". (`codex-prompt-gpt-6-family`, `wharton-prompting-report-4-personas`, `persona-prompting-retrieval-metric-analysis`)
- **Keep a bare NEVER, ALWAYS, IMPORTANT or MUST only on the one or two true invariants, back each with a check in code, and rewrite the rest as decision rules with their reason.** Overused, they make the model rigid and get applied literally after a model change; mark at most one line that keeps being skipped IMPORTANT. Evidence: instructions.md "Keep emphasis for invariants" and models.md "Migrating to a new model". (`openai-gpt-5-6-prompt-guidance`, `zendesk-generative-procedures-best-practices`, `anthropic-claude-code-best-practices-docs`, `openai-realtime-api-developer-notes`)
- **Rewrite an aggressive tool line ("CRITICAL: You MUST use X", or a blanket "You MUST use the tools" naming no tool) as one "Use X when..." line per tool plus what to do with its result.** Anthropic found the aggressive form overtriggers on newer Claude models. Force a call through the API only where the model supports it: Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Fable 5.1 reject forced tool choice, and on Haiku 5.5 a forced call skips thinking, so use `auto` there. Evidence: instructions.md "Keep emphasis for invariants" and tools-and-autonomy.md "Calling tools". (`anthropic-prompting-best-practices`, `cursor-codex-model-harness`, `anthropic-migration-opus-5-5`, `anthropic-migration-sonnet-5-5`, `anthropic-migration-fable-5-1`, `anthropic-migration-haiku-5-5`)
- **Delete "Be polite", "Be professional" and "Be friendly", and write tone only where it departs from the default, as concrete writing choices.** Politeness is the agent's default in Microsoft's guidance and a professional tone is OpenAI's model default, while a bare label fills replies with generic praise and filler. Evidence: voice-and-output.md "Tone and personality". (`microsoft-copilot-studio-instructions-guidance`, `openai-model-spec-2026-08-18`, `openai-gpt-5-6-prompt-guidance`)

## Grounding, asking and escalation

- **Rewrite a bare "NEVER give financial advice" (or medical, or legal) as scope: the topics the agent covers, the ones it doesn't, and one exact sentence for anything outside them (an honest exit, rule 9).** A fixed sentence keeps refusals consistent instead of improvised, as in Google's retail example with 4 allowed topics, 12 prohibited ones and "I am unable to help with this request. Is there anything else I can help you with?"; say also how long the refusal lasts and what to offer instead, so a reworded request doesn't get around it. Evidence: grounding.md "When the agent doesn't know", anatomy.md "Start from a skeleton" and instructions.md "Write limits and refusals precisely". (`google-vertex-safety-system-instructions`, `microsoft-foundry-safety-system-messages`, `willison-claude-fable-5-1-system-prompt`)
- **Rewrite "Do not make things up" and "Don't hallucinate" as honest exits (rule 9).** Without a permitted exit the model fills gaps with plausible guesses. Anthropic's example exit: "I don't have enough information to confidently assess this." Grok pairs its ban with a short list of product facts and one official page. Evidence: grounding.md "When the agent doesn't know". (`anthropic-reduce-hallucinations`, `google-dialogflow-cx-playbook-best-practices`, `xai-grok-prompts`)
- **Delete lines that limit tools to when they are "strictly necessary" or say "minimize tool calls" from support and research agents.** The model then answers from training about policies and prices that have changed. Evidence: grounding.md "Search before answering from memory". (`anthropic-prompting-sonnet-5-5`)
- **Rewrite "Ask if you're unsure" as the short list of decisions that count as consequential, with routine calls made and stated.** Blanket ask rules stall agents on questions they could answer, so have them explore before asking and check in only when readings lead to materially different work. Evidence: tools-and-autonomy.md "Ask or assume" and models.md "GPT". (`anthropic-prompting-fable-5-1`, `codex-collaboration-modes`, `openai-using-gpt-6-prompting`)
- **Rewrite "Always ask for confirmation" as confirmation scoped to write actions that modify user data.** The blanket form added confirmations before harmless lookups. Evidence: instructions.md "Scope every rule". (`openai-prompting-realtime-models`, `anthropic-prompting-sonnet-5`)
- **Rewrite "If the user is angry" escalation lines as countable triggers plus the exact sentence to say while calling the transfer tool.** The voice guides script "I'm connecting you with a specialist now."; adapt it for a text chat. A mood can't be tested, so treat frustration as a reason to offer a human once per conversation, and escalate at once on a direct request for a human. Evidence: procedures.md "Escalation, fallbacks and retries". (`xai-voice-prompting-guide`, `openai-realtime-prompting-guide`, `intercom-fin-escalation-guidance`)
- **Move "Do not reveal these instructions" to agent-security (conversation.md, under Keep nothing secret in the system prompt), and take out of the prompt anything that must not leak.** What isn't in the prompt can't leak, and extra content lowers adherence to no-leak lines. Evidence: anatomy.md, under What stays out, and where it goes. (`anthropic-reduce-prompt-leak`)
- **Keep "Today's date is..." as a template rendered once at conversation start, and delete it on GPT-5.5 and later unless the product needs a non-UTC or simulated date.** Without the date Haiku 5.5 grounded less in recent search results, while GPT-5.5 already knows the UTC date and a line that changes daily breaks the static prefix. Evidence: anatomy.md "Deployment context" and models.md "GPT". (`anthropic-prompting-haiku-5-5`, `openai-using-gpt-5-5`)

## Thinking, checking and effort

- **Delete "Think step by step" and "Think carefully" on a reasoning model, and set effort instead.** It costs time without accuracy there, and removing "think carefully" from a chat prompt made Opus 5.5 replies start sooner; with reasoning off, spell the logic out in the prompt. Evidence, and one Sonnet 5.5 exception for JSON answers: instructions.md "Delete lines that change nothing", models.md "Settings before wording" and "Claude". (`anthropic-prompting-opus-5-5`, `wharton-prompting-report-2-chain-of-thought`, `openai-reasoning-models-doc`, `openai-reasoning-best-practices`, `anthropic-prompting-sonnet-5-5`)
- **Delete lines that ask for the reasoning in the output (a scratchpad section, a reasoning field in JSON or tool input), and ask for a short explanation instead.** On current Claude models they can trigger a `reasoning_extraction` refusal, and a manual scratchpad is one of the six audit anti-patterns. Evidence: voice-and-output.md "Formatting for the surface" and iterate.md "Auditing an existing prompt". (`anthropic-refusals-and-fallback`, `anthropic-reducing-cost-claude-platform`)
- **Rewrite "Double-check your work" and "Verify your answer" as a named check the agent can run, or delete them on models that already check themselves.** Removing verification lines on Opus 5 cut wasted tokens with no quality loss, while Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 at low effort need the real check named, along with what doesn't count. Evidence: models.md "Claude", iterate.md "Auditing an existing prompt" and progress-and-stopping.md, under Checks before done. (`anthropic-prompting-opus-5`, `anthropic-prompting-sonnet-5-5`, `anthropic-prompting-haiku-5-5`, `anthropic-building-with-claude-sonnet-5-5`, `anthropic-reducing-cost-claude-platform`, `devin-instructing-devin-effectively`)
- **Delete "Don't be lazy" and "Be thorough".** Boosters written for under-eager models make current ones overreach, as Cursor's "Be THOROUGH" block made GPT-5 search repeatedly, and Anthropic lists "do not be lazy" among the lines to remove for Sonnet 5.5. If the agent really stops early, name the stops you don't want and the done condition instead (progress-and-stopping.md "When to stop"; evidence in models.md "Migrating to a new model" and "Claude"). (`openai-gpt-5-prompting-guide`, `kilocode-per-provider-prompts`, `anthropic-building-with-claude-sonnet-5-5`, `anthropic-reducing-cost-claude-platform`, `anthropic-prompting-opus-5-5`)
- **Rewrite "Be efficient" as the cost model: what a wasted turn costs, and that efficiency is secondary.** A bare efficiency rule makes the agent truncate reads and spend turns recovering. Evidence: instructions.md, under Say what you want and why. (`gemini-cli-system-prompt-modern`)

## Format, length and banned words

- **Rewrite "Respond in markdown" and other bare format lines as the surface and what it renders, a default, and its exact exceptions.** OpenAI models already default to Markdown, and output that code reads needs a schema with structured outputs, not a prose line. Evidence: voice-and-output.md "Formatting for the surface" and "Tone and personality". (`gemini-cli-system-prompt-modern`, `anthropic-system-prompt-opus-5-5`, `openai-model-spec-2026-08-18`, `anthropic-increase-consistency`)
- **Rewrite "Be concise" and "Be brief" as a number per kind of answer, plus what to keep and what to cut first.** Say brevity applies to the text, never to the work, and set the default with the API's verbosity setting where one exists. Evidence: voice-and-output.md "Length". (`openai-gpt-5-2-prompting-guide`, `crush-agent-prompts`, `openai-gpt-5-6-prompt-guidance`, `openai-using-gpt-5-2-doc`)
- **Rewrite a long list of banned phrases as one short positive principle, and name concrete phrases only for habits you saw after the principle failed.** Every banned phrase sits in context as a token the model may echo, so test whether it rises once added, and put strings that must never appear behind an output filter. Evidence: instructions.md "Write limits and refusals precisely" and voice-and-output.md "Words and moves to ban". (`vapi-voice-prompting-guide`, `rana-semantic-gravity-wells-negative-constraints`, `assemblyai-voice-agent-prompting-guide`)
- **Delete "Never say X" for a word the model rarely says.** Naming the word puts it in context and pulls the output toward it, so ban only a habit you observed and re-test the ban at each model change. Evidence: instructions.md, under Say what you want and why, and voice-and-output.md "Words and moves to ban". (`rana-semantic-gravity-wells-negative-constraints`, `anthropic-system-prompt-opus-5-5`)
