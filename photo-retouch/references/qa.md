# QA gates

The gate tools live in `templates/portrait-qa/tools/` (`compare_masters.py`, `compare.py` and `eyes_bench.py` in `templates/pipeline/tools/`); each tool's docstring holds its thresholds. Keep the numbers when you copy a tool.

Edge gate, edge montage and color zones read their inputs from `work/gate/`, which `tools/gate_renders.py <photo>:<style> ...` writes: run it before them, after every render they judge. It renders again through portrait-qa's own style renderer (`build_scene`, `edit_wall`, `fit_params` in its `recipe/styles.py`), so these three gates apply to a project built on that renderer. In a project built on `templates/pipeline/`, contours are checked with `tools/backdrop_sheet.py` (each head's edge, zoomed at native), `tools/backdrop_zone.py` (people pixels the background changed) and the wall-only check below. Edge montage and color zones also read the camera's embedded JPEG through `camera_original` in `recipe/output.py`, which writes `work/<photo>-camara.jpg`, so they don't run under `.sin-jpg`.

- **Edge gate** (`edge_gate.py <photo> <style> final`, the stage the other two gates read): normal profiles every 8 px along the contour, the edit against the same render with the wall unbrightened, per class (skin, dark, mixed). It is the checkable form of "contours identical to the camera original": edge defects are sub-pixel and invisible at fit.
- **Color zones** (`color_zones.py`), for what a style leaves at the camera's color: fixed face zones against the camera JPEG. Register it to the RAW develop per crop first (phase correlation): it is offset ~3 px, and unregistered comparisons show fake differences at edges. A style that moves skin toward a reference is judged by [measurement.md](measurement.md) > Tolerances instead.
- **Gradual corrections:** at most 0.09 EV between neighbouring pixels at a correction's border. Steeper transitions showed as visible steps. For the under-eyes, `tools/eyes_bench.py cache <photo>`, then `hoja <photo>`, prints it as `contorno` (EV/px); all 16 eyes held it at 100 %. For a retouch correction (one that shows between the `work/` intermediates), `tools/compare.py <photo> x0 y0 x1 y1 <out> --diff` on a crop of its border, with no healed spot inside, prints it as `contorno`. Grade, style and backdrop corrections leave no intermediate, and no template tool measures their border yet: look at it at 200 %, on `edge_montage.py` where it meets a contour, for a line where the correction stops.
- **Wall-only changes leave people untouched:** after one, `tools/compare_masters.py respaldos/<version>-antes-<change> --mask proceso-notas/estilos/<photo>-persona.npz` reports 0 % of pixels changed inside the mask, run once per photo the change touched, reading that photo's lines. Write the mask first: an `.npz` with the key `alpha`, 1 where the A channel of `editadas/fondo/<photo>-capa.png` is 65535 (alpha 1 in the native matte `recipe/backdrop.py` writes from `recipe/matte.py`), 0 elsewhere: edge pixels under alpha 1 take their wall share of the change by design ([compositing.md](compositing.md)). Without `--mask` the tool reports one box per file, and a wall change stretches it over the people.
